3) AMEX ATM REVERSAL
Full reversal / no dispense:
- MTI 1420 -> 1430.
- Bit 24 = 400 Full reversal.
- Bit 4 = 000000000000.
- Bit 11 = mismo STAN del 1200.
- Bit 25 = 4017 malfunction/no cash dispensed o 4018 timeout al retirar efectivo.
- Bit 30 = importe original + 12 ceros.
- Bit 31 = TID recibido en el 1210.
- 1430 Bit 39 = 400 Reversal Accepted.

Partial reversal:
- MTI 1420 -> 1430.
- Bit 24 = 401 Partial reversal.
- Bit 4 = importe realmente dispensado.
- Bit 25 = 4004 Completed partially.
- Bit 30 = importe original.
- Bit 31 = TID original.
