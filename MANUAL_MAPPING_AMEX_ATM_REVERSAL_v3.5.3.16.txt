AMEX ATM REVERSAL - MANUAL MAPPING

Network Specifications - Authorization, October 2023:
- ATM Reversal Advice 1420 is used when ATM fails to dispense, partially dispenses,
  Cardmember cancels before cash dispense, ATM times out, or communications outage causes timeout.
- Bit 4 = zero on non-dispense; actual dispensed amount on partial dispense.
- Bit 11 = same STAN as original 1200.
- Bit 24 = 400 Full reversal / 401 Partial reversal.
- Bit 25 mandatory Message Reason Code.
- Bit 30 mandatory Original Amount N(24).
- Bit 31 reuses TID returned in original 1210, when present.
- 1430 acknowledges receipt of 1420 and does not alone mean account exposure has already been reversed.

Codes Reference Guide, October 2023:
- 4004 Completed partially.
- 4017 Suspected malfunction / no cash dispensed.
- 4018 ATM-only timed-out at taking money / no cash dispensed.
